Do you remember how, in 2012 or so, we started hearing about this little technology called WebGL, and how it could take us back to the insanely creative, Flash-like aesthetic of the early 2000s? And then we all collectively forgot about it and went back to maintaining our corporate cookie-cutter Bootstrap websites for 15 years?

I just now remembered about that and am discovering some crazy amazing things like [this](https://paveldogreat.github.io/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation/). What have you discovered?
