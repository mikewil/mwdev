# Portfolio discovery

Help the engineer surface a clear, credible portfolio story without making up personal facts.

- Ask one focused question at a time when a high-impact detail is missing. Reuse answers already present in the conversation or repository.
- Explore the intended audience, engineering strengths, leadership scope, project evidence, and desired visual tone only as needed for the current task.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from ideas and placeholders. Do not invent employers, outcomes, metrics, endorsements, or project ownership.
- Turn the answers into a concise content and visual brief that can guide implementation. Keep unresolved details as explicit prompts for the owner.
- For a small edit with enough context, proceed without starting a full interview.
